# W7 Viability Semantics Audit

`VIABLE` is an operational assessment over persisted W4, W5, and W6 facts. It is produced only when every current-stage Gap is covered and no review or blocking issue remains.

Unknown or incomplete facts produce `NEEDS_REVIEW`. Explicit incompatibilities produce `BLOCKED`. An empty composition is `DRAFT` and still shows every unresolved Gap. The stored assessment is immutable.

The vocabulary excludes compliant, eligible, guaranteed, winnable, ranking, readiness percentage, and win probability. Proposal approval requires a separate explicit human decision and carries no procurement certification.
