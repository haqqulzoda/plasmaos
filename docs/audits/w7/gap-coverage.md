# W7 Gap Coverage Audit

Each revision snapshots every exact W4 Gap together with its latest review assertion, effective W4 coverage, review state, and resolution category.

Candidate coverage requires a current exact chain, `SHORTLISTED`, `SUPPORTED_BY_EVIDENCE`, explicit reviewed contribution authority, current availability, confirmed participation, and an acceptable assignment window. A partial or evidence missing candidate produces partial/review outcomes and cannot be combined algorithmically with another partial candidate.

Later stage obligations are disclosed separately. Not applicable and W4 supported items need no W7 candidate. Company evidence, clarification, and human interpretation remain unresolved until W4 changes them. W7 writes no W4 row.
