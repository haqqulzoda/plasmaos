# W8 Tenant Isolation Audit

Workspace, pack, item, and artifact rows carry Organization and Pursuit lineage. Queries require both values; composite foreign keys prevent relationship substitution. Seal/export/download validate active Membership inside the service in addition to route dependencies.

Candidate identity may be `NETWORK_SHARED`, but its selection, W6 participation, scenario, pack, and export remain Organization-private. Cross-Organization pack lookup returns no row and cross-Organization sealing fails before a snapshot can be created.

Artifacts use Organization/Pursuit/pack scoped opaque storage keys. No cross-tenant content deduplication identifier is exposed. The permanent test also revokes Membership and proves artifact resolution is denied.
