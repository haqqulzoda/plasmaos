# W8 Team Evidence Audit

The roster comes only from the exact approved W7 revision. Each Partner Firm or Expert retains candidate identity, proposed contributions, W5 qualification/evidence state, evidence identities, W6 fact IDs and effective states, confirmation date/source, reconfirmation limit, conditions, and assignment window.

Project references preserve role, dates, completion, value, currency, and `value_basis`. A contract or consortium total is not transformed into Firm share. References without document bytes are labeled reviewed structured references.

CV items preserve immutable CVVersion ID/SHA and structured facts. They are labeled `STRUCTURED_CV_FACTS`, with `source_cv_document_available=false`, `candidate_signed=false`, and `original_cv=false` when source bytes are absent. Private notes and unrelated histories are excluded.
