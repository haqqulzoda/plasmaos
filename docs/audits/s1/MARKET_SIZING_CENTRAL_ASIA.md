## Changes since v2

v2 (2026-10-11) and v3 cover the same window (award notices to 2026-10-10). Three changes move the figures:

1. **Notice-country validation.** 9 notices whose whole award section belongs to another country's procurement (foreign bidders priced in a foreign currency on genuine packages of the project country) are excluded; they stay in the ledger, flagged (see "Notice-country validation" below). No notice sits on another country's project.
2. **Name normalizer.** Uzbek, Russian and English legal forms written in words (MAS'ULIYATI CHEKLANGAN JAMIYATI, AKSIYADORLIK JAMIYATI, XUSUSIY KORXONA, OBSHCHESTVO S OGRANICHENNOY OTVETSTVENNOSTYU, AKTSIONERNOE OBSHCHESTVO, LIMITED LIABILITY PARTNERSHIP, JOINT STOCK COMPANY...) are removed before grouping, and Cyrillic names are transliterated, so ООО «X», X MChJ and X LLC are one firm. Whole ledger: 3,925 → 3,915 firms.
3. **Duplicate award notices.** A notice whose every award (package reference and title, awarded firm, award date, published price) is published again in another notice is counted once: 20 notices in the four countries, 4 of them Uzbekistan (e.g. OP00424867 = OP00425438, one audit, USD 33,000). They stay in the ledger, flagged.

The 2026-10-12 re-ingest also stored three Tajikistan goods notices re-published with changed text (version 2 each); reads use the newest version.

## Method

- Source: World Bank procurement-notice API, notice type "Contract Award", project country Uzbekistan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan or Kazakhstan, every procurement group, notice date inside the window. Each notice is stored verbatim (raw text, retrieval time, content hash) and parsed deterministically: the Awarded, Evaluated and Rejected sections list every firm the notice names; firms listed together before one score/price block are one joint-venture bid. Completeness was checked against the API per country and group.
- A bid is one proposal or bid on one contract. A firm is a normalized name (upper-case, punctuation, legal forms such as LLC/LTD/JSC and joint-venture role markers such as ", LEAD", "(JV)", "CONSORTIUM OF" removed) plus the firm's published country; a firm in a joint venture is counted as a bidder on that bid.
- Individual consultants are excluded from every figure and reported only as a count. Names that are a public body or a project (ministries, PIUs, implementation units, committees, bodies "under" a ministry or agency, a bare "<sector> PROJECT") are left out of firm counts and listed for review; other names with PROJECT or AGENCY stay counted (many registered firms carry these words).
- Awarded value = the signed contract price, or the awarded bid's final evaluated price when no signed price is published; counted once per joint venture. Median award = median USD equivalent of awarded contracts with a usable published value.

## Caveats

- World Bank only. These layers are not in the ledger and are not estimated, so every figure is a floor:
  - Asian Development Bank (ADB)
  - European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD)
  - Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)
  - Islamic Development Bank (IsDB)
  - National procurement: Uzbekistan's state procurement portals (e.g. the UzEx exchange and xarid.uzex.uz) and the national systems of Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan and Kazakhstan
  - Other bilateral and multilateral financiers (e.g. KfW, JICA, AFD, EU-funded tenders)
- Only contracts whose award notice is published are counted; cancelled procurements and awards not yet published are missing.
- Expression-of-interest submissions are not published (consulting): firms that submitted an EOI but were not invited are invisible.
- Direct Selection and direct contracting awards have one firm and no competition; they are included.
- Kazakhstan's World Bank volume is small, so its figures rest on few notices.
- Name normalization can split one firm written two ways (see the merge-candidate review list), or (rarely) merge two firms with the same normalized name and country.
- USD equivalents use one date's rates for awards signed over two years; they size the market, they are not the dollar value at signing.
