# W5 Candidate Evaluation

Retrieval relevance and qualification are separate steps. Term overlap orders a bounded lead set. It is not exposed as a qualification probability.

Firm evaluation considers exact Requirement terms, explicit capabilities, and reviewed ProjectReferences. Comparable-contract predicates count only reviewed or verified completed references. Value, share, role, completion, and value basis remain visible evidence facts.

Expert evaluation considers the exact Position and the latest immutable CV version. Education, assignment, language, certification, and specialization facts are cited. Unreviewed or absent CV proof results in `EVIDENCE_MISSING`. Unproven criteria result in `PARTIAL` or `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

Candidate states are distinct from W4 coverage. A CandidateMatch never changes a Requirement, Position, Gap, or assertion. Human decisions append separate rows and may correct only the proposed W5 contribution.
