# W7 TeamScenario Input Contract

## Stable W6 projection

For each independent participation root, W7 may consume:

- exact CandidateMatch, CandidateSearchRun, Gap, and W4 AnalysisRun IDs;
- Firm or Expert identity and proposed contribution snapshot;
- W5 qualification/evidence state and effective shortlist decision;
- latest recorded and effective availability, window, effort/capacity, source, observation, validity, and assignment window coverage;
- latest recorded and effective interest, conditions, source, observation, and validity;
- latest recorded and effective participation state, confirmation source, observation, conditions, reason, reconfirm boundary, and freshness;
- upstream stale flag and reason;
- same candidate participation record IDs in the same Pursuit.

## Required W7 decisions

W7 may create a DRAFT scenario with tentative, missing, expired, or unreconfirmed inputs only when those facts are disclosed. Before calling a scenario ready or viable, W7 must evaluate current Gap coverage, evidence quality, contribution rules, current availability, participation confirmation, reconfirmation, assignment overlap, candidate double counting, and unresolved assumptions.

W7 must not infer readiness from shortlist, qualification, interest, availability, or confirmation alone. It must not mutate or rebind the W4, W5, or W6 histories.
