# P1 progressive-disclosure audit

## Result

- Confirmed upload context is compact by default; editing and source suggestions appear only when requested. Conflicts remain visible and open the relevant field for review without overwriting it.
- The exact analysis-pack chooser is collapsed after a reviewable result exists and expands when no result exists.
- Requirement details expand into source, assessment, organization evidence, and review controls.
- Candidate participation forms, histories, team issues, decisions, and revisions remain explicit disclosures.
- Team options are not rendered until at least one participation record exists. With no reviewed partner/expert need, the Team tab shows a single explanatory empty state.
- Proposal packs lead with summary counts and matrix; roster, references, structured CV facts, later-stage obligations, checklist, documents, and technical hashes are disclosed on demand.

This keeps the primary decision surface readable while preserving all existing review and audit authority.
