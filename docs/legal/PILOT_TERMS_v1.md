# Plasma Pilot Terms (version 1)

These terms apply between **Plasma AI Solutions**, Tashkent, Uzbekistan ("Plasma", "we") and the organization named in the company details of this checkout ("the Customer", "you") when you buy a Plasma Pilot, Pursuit Desk or an extra pursuit in the Plasma app.

## 1. What you buy

- **Pilot:** three months of Plasma from the day of activation: 6 pursuits, 1 partner introduction by our team, Expressions of Interest, your own evidence (company records, references, experts) and partner search. The price is USD 300 a month, USD 900 paid in advance for the three months.
- **Pursuit Desk:** twelve months of Plasma from the day of activation: 24 pursuits and 2 partner introductions a plan year, with the features of the Pilot. USD 6,000 a year.
- **Extra pursuit:** one more pursuit in your current plan period. USD 200.

A *pursuit* is one tender you analyse in Plasma. It counts once, when its first analysis starts; analysing the same tender again (an amendment, a new document set) does not count again. Browsing and saving tenders is unlimited.

## 2. Activation and payment

- **Card:** paid through our payment provider, Polar. The plan starts when the payment is confirmed.
- **Bank transfer:** we issue an invoice (in UZS at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan rate of the issue date for Uzbek residents, in USD otherwise). The plan starts immediately. If the invoice is unpaid ten days after it was issued, your organization returns to the free Explore plan until the payment arrives; your data is kept.
- Prices exclude bank charges on your side.

## 3. Pilot credit

The amount you paid for the Pilot is credited once, to your first annual invoice (for example, Pursuit Desk). The credit applies to one annual order only and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## 4. Refund guarantee

If the Pilot does not work for you, tell us within 30 days of the payment and we refund it in full. After a refund the organization returns to the free Explore plan.

## 5. Your data

- Documents you upload, your company records and your analyses belong to you and are visible only to the members of your organization and, for support, to Plasma's team.
- We do not send anything to third parties on your behalf. Partner introductions are made by our team only after you ask for them, and only with the partner's consent.
- When the plan ends, your data is kept and stays readable; you can ask us to delete it.

## 6. What Plasma is and is not

Plasma reads public tender documents and your own records and shows requirements, gaps and drafts with their sources. It supports your bid decisions; it does not decide for you and does not guarantee that a bid is compliant or that it wins. Check every requirement against the official tender documents before you submit.

## 7. Ending

You can stop using Plasma at any time. A Pilot ends after its three months; a Pursuit Desk renews each year unless you cancel it before the renewal date. We may suspend an organization that misuses the service or does not pay, after telling you.
