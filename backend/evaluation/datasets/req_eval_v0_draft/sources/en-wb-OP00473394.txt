Request for Quotations (RFQ)

Software Licenses for Research, Analytics and Institutional Productivity

Project Number: P504420

Project Name: “Innovative Social Protection System for Inclusion of Vulnerable People” (INSON)

Financing Sources: IDA Credit No. 7555-UZ

Purchaser: National Agency for Social Protection (NASP) under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

Contract Reference and Subject: UZ-INSON-RFQ-3.2.3/R - Software Licenses for Research, Analytics and Institutional Productivity.

The Republic of Uzbekistan has received financing from the World Bank (hereinafter referred to as “the Bank”) toward the cost of the project Innovative Social Protection System for Inclusion of Vulnerable People (INSON) and intends to apply part of the proceeds toward payments under the contract for the supply of Software Licenses for Research, Analytics and Institutional Productivity.

The Purchaser hereby invites eligible Suppliers to submit quotations for the supply of software licenses and subscriptions in accordance with the Request for Quotations (RFQ) document. The procurement comprises 13 separate items. Suppliers may submit quotations for one, several, or all items. Each item will be evaluated and awarded separately.

The procurement will be conducted through the Request for Quotations (RFQ) method, as specified in the World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers, Fifth Edition, September 2023, and is open to all eligible Suppliers as defined in the Regulations.

Award will be made separately for each item to the lowest evaluated substantially responsive Supplier, in accordance with the requirements specified in the RFQ.

The RFQ documents are available in English and Russian. In case of any discrepancy between the Russian and English versions, the Russian version shall prevail. The following link should be accessed for the complete RFQ documents, including the technical specifications and quotation forms:
