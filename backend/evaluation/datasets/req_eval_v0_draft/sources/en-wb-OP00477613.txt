REPUBLIC OF TAJIKISTAN

REQUEST FOR EXPRESSIONS OF INTEREST

FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AUDIT

OF OJSC “Pamir Energy Company”

FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31,2025

Established as part of a public-private partnership (PPP) between the Government of the Republic of Tajikistan and OJSC “Pamir Energy Company” (PE), a subsidiary of the Aga Khan Fund for Economic Development (AKFED), PE is an integrated energy utility company operating in the region of Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Oblast (GBAO) in Eastern Tajikistan.

The objective of these consulting services (“the Services”) is to conduct financial audits for fiscal year ending December 31, 2025, for the OJSC “Pamir Energy Company” entity. The auditor shall express an opinion on the financial statements of OJSC “Pamir Energy Company” prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) as published by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB). The duration of the assignment is expected to commence from Mach 1, 2026 and ended June 30, 2026.

The OJSC “Pamir Energy Company” assigned for selection and recruitment of an audit firm, now invites eligible consulting firms (“Consultants”) to indicate their interest in providing the Services. Interested Consultants should provide information demonstrating that they have the required qualifications and relevant experience to perform the Services. The shortlisting criteria are:

Relevant experience in similar assignments (including in the audit of companies);

Technical and managerial capabilities of firm;

Company profile;

Financial capabilities of firm.

The Consultants shall have a valid license from Ministry of Finance of Republic of Tajikistan for u undertaking the Services.
