1. The Government of the Kyrgyz Republic has received financing from the World Bank toward the cost of the Kyrgyz Renewable Energy Development Project (KRED) and intends to apply part of the proceeds toward payments under the Contract for Design, Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning of the 220/110/10 kV “Isanova” Substation and Associated 110/35/10 kV Substation Extensions (Kara Shoro, Karatay and Kara Kuldzha), including Associated Transmission Line Connection and Interface Works.

2. The Ministry of Energy of the Kyrgyz Republic, through its Project Management Office (PMO), now invites sealed Bids from eligible Bidders for the Design, Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning of the 220/110/10 kV “Isanova” Substation and Associated 110/35/10 kV Substation Extensions, including Associated Transmission Line Connection and Interface Works, under a single lump-sum Design, Supply and Installation (DSI) Contract.

3. Bidding will be conducted through international competitive procurement using a Request for Bids (RFB), Single-Stage, Two-Envelope procedure, as specified in the World Bank Procurement Regulations for IPF Borrowers, Fifth Edition, September 2023 (the “Procurement Regulations”) and is open to all eligible Bidders as defined in the Procurement Regulations. In addition, please refer to the Procurement Regulations setting forth the World Bank’s policy on conflict of interest.

4. Interested eligible Bidders may obtain further information from the Project Management Office (PMO), Ministry of Energy of the Kyrgyz Republic, Attn.: Mr. [REDACTED], Director, E-mail: [REDACTED], and inspect the bidding documents during office hours from 09:00 to 17:00 hours (Bishkek Time) at the address given below.

5. The bidding document in English may be obtained free of charge by interested eligible Bidders upon submission of a written request to the address below. The bidding documents will be transmitted electronically.

6. Bids must be delivered to the address below on or before 14:00 (Bishkek Time) on December 09, 2026. Electronic bidding will not be permitted. Late Bids will be rejected. The outer Bid envelopes marked “ORIGINAL BID”, and the inner envelopes marked “TECHNICAL PART” will be publicly opened at 14:00 (Bishkek Time) on December 09, 2026 at the address below in the presence of the Bidders’ designated representatives and anyone who chooses to attend. All envelopes marked “FINANCIAL PART” shall remain unopened and will be held in safe custody of the Employer until the second public Bid opening.

7. All Bids must be accompanied by a Bid Security of United States Dollars Four Hundred Thousand (USD 400,000), or an equivalent amount in a freely convertible currency.

8. Attention is drawn to the Procurement Regulations requiring the Borrower to disclose information on the successful bidder’s beneficial ownership, as part of the Contract Award Notice, using the Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Form as included in the bidding document
