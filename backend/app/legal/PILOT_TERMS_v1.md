# Plasma Pilot and Subscription Terms

**Version 1 · effective 26 October 2026**

These terms are an agreement between **Plasma AI Solutions**, Tashkent, Uzbekistan ("**Plasma**", "**we**") and the organisation named in the order ("**the Customer**", "**you**"). They apply when you buy a Pilot, Pursuit Desk, a Per-tender review or Extra pursuits, online in the Plasma app or by signing the order at the end of this document. Agent and Enterprise plans use these terms together with a separate order form.

Accepting these terms online ("I accept") or signing the order has the same effect. The person accepting confirms they are authorised to bind the Customer.

## 1. What you buy

| Plan | Term | Price | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | 3 months from activation | USD 900 (USD 300 a month, paid in advance) | 6 pursuits · unlimited users · onboarding done for you · 2 specialist reviews · 1 partner or expert introduction · success criteria and a joint review at the end |
| **Pursuit Desk** | 12 months from activation, renews yearly | USD 6,000 a year | 24 pursuits a plan year · unlimited users · onboarding done for you · 1 specialist review a quarter · 2 introductions a plan year |
| **Per-tender review** | One pursuit | USD 400 | One pursuit end to end and a 30-minute review call |
| **Extra pursuit** | Current plan period | USD 200 each | One additional pursuit |

Every paid plan includes: finding tenders from all sources Plasma covers; requirements quoted from the tender documents; eligibility gaps checked against your records; Expressions of Interest drafted in the lender's format; your projects, experts and CVs matched to the criteria; and partner and expert search.

A **pursuit** is one tender you actively work on in Plasma. It counts once, when its first analysis starts. Re-analysing the same tender (for example after an amendment) does not count again. Browsing and saving tenders is unlimited. Unused pursuits do not carry over to the next plan period.

Features that Plasma describes as "Coming" are not part of what you buy until they are released.

## 2. Onboarding and services

- **Onboarding.** Within 7 business days of receiving your documents, our team loads up to 20 project references and up to 10 experts or CVs into your workspace and checks them with you.
- **Specialist reviews.** A Plasma procurement specialist reviews the requirements and gaps of a pursuit you choose and discusses them with you.
- **Introductions.** When you ask, our team looks for a firm or expert that can cover a requirement you cannot meet alone and introduces you, only with the other party's consent. We do not guarantee that a suitable partner exists, agrees or performs; any agreement with a partner is between you and that partner.
- **Support.** By e-mail (hq@plasmaos.uz) and in the app on business days in Tashkent, normally within one business day.

## 3. Pilot success criteria

At the kickoff meeting we agree written success criteria with you (for example: evidence library loaded, number of tenders analysed, staff time per EOI). In the last week of the Pilot we review them together. Shortlisting and award results are recorded when known but are not success criteria, because they depend on the competition and the client's evaluation.

## 4. Activation and payment

- **Card.** You pay through our payment provider **Polar**, which acts as the seller of record for card payments and issues the receipt. The plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed.
- **Bank transfer.** We issue an invoice from Plasma AI Solutions: in Uzbek soums at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan rate of the invoice date for Uzbek residents, in US dollars otherwise. Your plan starts immediately. Payment is due within 7 days. If the invoice is still unpaid 10 days after it was issued, your organisation moves to the free Explore plan until payment arrives; your data is kept.
- **Taxes and charges.** Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT and other taxes where they apply, and bank charges on your side.

## 5. Pilot credit

The amount you paid for the Pilot is credited once, in full, to your first annual-plan payment, if you order an annual plan within 30 days after the Pilot ends. The credit applies to one order, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be transferred.

## 6. Refunds

- **Pilot: 30-day guarantee.** If you tell us within 30 days of activation that the Pilot is not working for you, we refund the full Pilot fee. Your organisation then moves to the free Explore plan.
- **Onboarding guarantee.** If we have not completed onboarding within 7 business days of receiving your documents, you may cancel and receive a full refund.
- **Annual plans.** Annual fees are not refundable after 14 days from activation, except where Plasma materially fails to provide the service and does not fix it within 30 days of your written notice, or where the law requires a refund.

## 7. Renewal and ending

- A Pilot ends after three months; it does not renew automatically.
- Pursuit Desk renews for another year unless you cancel before the renewal date in the app or by e-mail.
- Either party may end the agreement with 14 days' written notice. Fees already paid are refundable only as described in section 6.
- We may suspend access, after telling you, if fees are unpaid or if the service is misused (section 8). We will restore access once the issue is resolved.
- When a plan ends, your organisation moves to the free Explore plan and your data stays available to you to read and export. On request we delete it (section 9).

## 8. Your responsibilities and acceptable use

You agree to:

- give accurate company and billing details and keep your users' sign-in credentials secure;
- upload only documents and records you have the right to use;
- obtain the consent of each person whose CV or personal data you upload, as required by the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" and other applicable law, including consent to processing by the providers listed in section 9;
- use Plasma only for your own organisation's bids (or, for bid consultants, your clients' bids under your agreement with them);
- not resell or share access outside your organisation, copy or extract Plasma's data in bulk, attempt to break its security, or use it to prepare false or misleading submissions.

## 9. Your data and confidentiality

- **Ownership.** Documents you upload, your company records, and the analyses and drafts produced for you belong to you. Plasma keeps the right to the software, its methods and its own data (including its database of public tender and award records).
- **Visibility.** Your data is visible only to your organisation's members and, when needed for support or onboarding, to Plasma's team, who are bound by confidentiality.
- **Use.** We use your data only to provide and improve the service for you. We do not sell it, and Plasma does not use your documents to train AI models.
- **Service providers.** To run Plasma we use: a hosting provider for the application and database (servers outside Uzbekistan, in the European Union); Google's Gemini API to analyse documents; Cloudflare R2 for encrypted off-site backups; Resend for e-mail; and Polar for card payments. Your data may therefore be processed outside Uzbekistan. We keep this list current in our trust pack.
- **Deletion.** On request we delete your organisation's data from the live service within 30 days; copies in backups are removed as those backups expire, within five weeks.
- **Security.** We maintain a security programme built on ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and SOC 2 controls. Plasma is not yet certified or audited against these standards.
- **Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this agreement, during the agreement and for three years after it.

## 10. What Plasma is and is not

Plasma reads public tender documents and your own records, and shows requirements, gaps, matches and drafts with their sources. AI-generated results can be incomplete or wrong, and public records can contain errors. **You remain responsible for your bid decisions and for checking every requirement against the official tender documents before you submit.** Plasma does not submit bids, does not set or suggest prices, and does not guarantee that a bid is compliant, shortlisted or awarded.
